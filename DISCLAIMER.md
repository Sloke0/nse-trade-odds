# Disclaimer

**Please read this fully before using anything in this repository.**

## 1. Not 100% accurate

The probabilities, ranges, scenario trees and verdicts this tool produces are **estimates**, not predictions or guarantees. They are worked out from past price data plus judgement about news, and **they can be wrong, sometimes badly wrong**.

As a rough guide, treat the output as about **55% reliable**: only slightly better than a coin toss. **This figure is the author's working assumption, not a measured result.** The tool's real accuracy can only be known by scoring its logged predictions against what actually happened (see "Measuring accuracy" in the README), and until enough predictions have been scored, nobody knows the true figure. It may turn out higher or lower.

A statement like "62% chance of hitting the target" means that in past situations that looked similar, the target was hit about 62% of the time. It does **not** mean this trade will work. Markets can and do behave in ways that have never happened before.

## 4. Trading risks

- Trading equities, and especially **futures and options**, carries a high risk of loss. With leveraged or sold options, losses can exceed your capital. SEBI studies have found that a large majority of individual F&O traders lose money.
- Stop-losses can be skipped over by price gaps, so losses can be larger than planned.
- Data may be incomplete, delayed or wrong. News found automatically may be outdated or misread.
- Lot sizes, expiry days, margins, taxes and charges change. Verify current values with your broker and the exchange.
